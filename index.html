Executive portfolio brief

Scoring models explained

Weighted value / effort

Combines business value (45%), strategic alignment (35%), and risk reduction (20%), then divides by effort. Use when aligning priorities to portfolio strategy. All three ratings are 1–10.

RICE

Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Reach is people affected per consistent time period; impact is 0.25–3; confidence is a percentage. Use for comparing customer-facing opportunities.

WSJF

(Business value + urgency + risk reduction) ÷ job size. Use to balance value, time criticality, and risk reduction against effort. Ratings use a consistent 1–10 scale.

Scoring is relative and depends on the quality of your assumptions. Use consistent units across initiatives; validate mandatory commitments and dependencies separately.
